# Norwegian Sentences 1–50

Sentences I asked about, with translations and the new vocabulary from each.

## 1

**Det handler om hjemmet ditt**
It's about your home.

New words:
- **handle om** — to be about / to concern
- **hjemmet** — the home (definite of *et hjem*)
- **ditt** — your (neuter singular possessive)

## 2

**Uansett hvor eller hvordan du bor, gjør Huseierne det enklere, tryggere og billigere å eie bolig.**
No matter where or how you live, Huseierne makes it easier, safer and cheaper to own a home.

New words:
- **uansett** — regardless / no matter
- **hvordan** — how
- **å bo** — to live / to reside
- **enkel / enklere** — simple, easy / easier
- **tryggere** — safer
- **billig / billigere** — cheap / cheaper
- **å eie** — to own
- **bolig** — home / dwelling / property
- **Huseierne** — "the House Owners", the Norwegian homeowners' association

Grammar: the sentence opens with a subordinate clause, so V2 forces inversion —
*gjør Huseierne*, not *Huseierne gjør*. Comparatives take **-ere**.

## 3

**Som medlem får du råd, juridisk hjelp og rabatter på det du faktisk trenger som boligeier.**
As a member you get advice, legal help and discounts on what you actually need as a homeowner.

New words:
- **medlem** — member
- **råd** — advice
- **juridisk** — legal
- **rabatt / rabatter** — discount / discounts
- **faktisk** — actually / in fact
- **å trenge** — to need
- **boligeier** — homeowner

Grammar: *det du trenger* = "what/that which you need" — the relative *som* is
optional here and normally dropped.

## 4

**Dette bladet behandler fundamentering med ringmur og kryperom som ventileres med uteluft,**
This sheet covers foundations with a perimeter foundation wall and a crawl space ventilated with outdoor air,

New words:
- **et blad / bladet** — sheet, leaflet, data sheet (also: leaf; magazine)
- **å behandle** — to treat; here: to cover / deal with (a topic)
- **fundamentering** — foundation work
- **ringmur** — perimeter foundation wall (lit. "ring wall")
- **kryperom** — crawl space
- **å ventilere / ventileres** — to ventilate / is ventilated
- **uteluft** — outdoor air

Grammar: double determination (*dette* + definite *-et*); s-passive *ventileres*
= "is ventilated", equivalent to *som blir ventilert*; *som* is the relative
pronoun attaching to *kryperom*.

## 5

**Det er lagt spesiell vekt på å vise nødvendige tiltak for å unngå fuktskader på huset.**
Special emphasis has been placed on showing the necessary measures for avoiding moisture damage to the house.

New words:
- **å legge vekt på** — to emphasize, to place weight on (fixed expression)
- **vekt** — weight; emphasis
- **spesiell** — special, particular
- **å vise** — to show
- **nødvendig** — necessary
- **et tiltak / tiltak** — measure, action, step taken (zero plural)
- **å unngå** — to avoid
- **fukt** — moisture, damp
- **fuktskade / fuktskader** — moisture damage
- **hus / huset** — house / the house

Grammar: impersonal passive with dummy subject *det* — *er lagt* reads as English
present perfect ("has been placed"), not "is placed". *for å* + infinitive marks
purpose ("in order to"), versus bare *å* after the preposition *på*. *Tiltak* is a
neuter zero-plural noun; the plural shows on the adjective *nødvendige*.

## 6

**Ringmur med kryperom kan være et alternativ til golv direkte på grunnen ved kjellerløs fundamentering.**
A perimeter foundation wall with a crawl space can be an alternative to a floor laid directly on the ground when building without a cellar.

New words:
- **et alternativ** — an alternative
- **golv** (also **gulv**) — floor
- **grunn / grunnen** — ground, subsoil (also: reason)
- **direkte** — directly
- **ved** — here: in the case of, when it comes to
- **kjeller** — cellar, basement
- **kjellerløs** — cellar-less, without a basement

Grammar: **-løs** is a productive suffix = English *-less* (*arbeidsløs*,
*meningsløs*). **ved + noun** compresses a whole clause into a phrase — typical of
technical writing, usually unpacked into a clause in English. *Grunnen* is the
ground as structural base, versus *bakken*, the surface you walk on. *Golv direkte
på grunnen* is the fixed technical term for "slab on grade".

## 7

**Løsningen er aktuell på flate og småkuperte tomter og i framkant av bygninger i skrånende terreng.**
The solution is applicable on flat and gently undulating plots, and at the front of buildings on sloping terrain.

New words:
- **en løsning / løsningen** — solution
- **aktuell** — relevant, applicable, current
- **flat** — flat
- **kupert / småkupert** — hilly, undulating / gently undulating
- **en tomt / tomter** — plot, site, lot
- **framkant** — front edge; **i framkant av** — at the front of
- **en bygning / bygninger** — building
- **å skråne / skrånende** — to slope / sloping
- **terreng** — terrain

Grammar: *aktuell* is a false friend — relevant/applicable, never "actual" (that
is *faktisk*). *Skrånende* is a present participle used adjectivally (*-ende*);
Norwegian uses these as adjectives only, never to build a continuous tense.

## 8

**Fundamentering med golv direkte på grunnen er generelt et bedre alternativ der forholdene ligger til rette for det.**
A foundation with the floor directly on the ground is generally a better option where conditions allow for it.

New words:
- **generelt** — generally
- **god → bedre → best** — good → better → best (irregular)
- **der** — where (relative)
- **et forhold / forholdene** — condition, circumstance; also relationship, ratio
- **å ligge til rette for** — to be favourable for, to allow for (idiom)

Grammar: *bedre* is irregular, unlike the *-ere* comparatives (*enklere*,
*billigere*); compare *mange → flere*, *liten → mindre*. *Der* introduces a
relative clause about a place or situation, while *hvor* is the question word.
*Ligge til rette for* is a set phrase — "conditions permit it" — and must be
learned whole.

## 9

**Fuktskader på undersiden av golvkonstruksjonen som følge av for fuktig klima i kryperommet, er svært utbredt.**
Moisture damage to the underside of the floor structure, caused by an overly humid climate in the crawl space, is very widespread.

New words:
- **underside / undersiden** — underside
- **golvkonstruksjon** — floor structure
- **som følge av** — as a result of, due to
- **for** + adjective — too (*for fuktig* = too damp)
- **fuktig** — damp, humid
- **klima** — climate
- **svært** — very, extremely
- **utbredt** — widespread, common

Grammar: *for* + adjective means "too", not "for" — a false friend to file beside
*aktuell*. *Altfor fuktig* = "far too damp".

## 10

**På trebjelkelag arter skadene seg som soppskader.**
In timber joist floors the damage manifests itself as fungal damage.

New words:
- **trebjelkelag** — timber joist floor (*tre* + *bjelke* + *lag*)
- **en bjelke** — beam, joist
- **å arte seg som** — to manifest itself as, to take the form of
- **en skade / skadene** — damage, injury
- **sopp** — fungus, mould; mushroom
- **soppskade** — fungal damage, rot

Grammar: V2 inversion once more — the fronted prepositional phrase pushes the
subject *skadene* behind the verb. *Arte seg* is reflexive; the *seg* is
obligatory, like *føle seg*, *befinne seg*, *vise seg*.

## 11

**Lettbetongelementer kan få korrosjon på armeringen med påfølgende avskalling av lettbetongen som dekker armeringen.**
Lightweight concrete elements can develop corrosion in the reinforcement, with subsequent spalling of the lightweight concrete that covers the reinforcement.

New words:
- **lettbetong** — lightweight (aerated) concrete
- **et element / elementer** — element, prefabricated unit
- **korrosjon** — corrosion
- **armering / armeringen** — reinforcement, rebar
- **påfølgende** — subsequent, ensuing
- **avskalling** — spalling, flaking off
- **å dekke** — to cover

## 12

**For å unngå skader er det derfor avgjørende at luftfuktigheten i kryperommet holdes nede på et forsvarlig nivå.**
To avoid damage it is therefore crucial that the air humidity in the crawl space is kept down at a safe level.

New words:
- **derfor** — therefore
- **avgjørende** — crucial, decisive
- **luftfuktighet / luftfuktigheten** — air humidity
- **å holde nede** — to keep down
- **forsvarlig** — safe, sound, acceptable
- **et nivå** — level

Grammar: fronted *for å*-phrase triggers V2 (*er det*). The s-passive *holdes*
sits inside an *at*-clause — the register's way of stating a requirement without
naming who must meet it. *-lig* and *-ende* build adjectives off verbs:
*forsvare → forsvarlig*, *avgjøre → avgjørende*.

## 13

**Tilgangen på fuktighet begrenses ved å:**
The ingress of moisture is limited by:

New words:
- **tilgang / tilgangen** — access, supply; here: ingress
- **fuktighet** — moisture, humidity
- **å begrense / begrenses** — to limit / is limited
- **ved å** — by (doing something)

Grammar: *ved å* + infinitive = "by doing", the counterpart of *ved* + noun from
sentence 6. The colon introduces a list of bare infinitives.

## 14

**Her finner du nyttig informasjon om isolering og tetting av tak til bygg og boliger.**
Here you'll find useful information about insulating and sealing roofs for buildings and homes.

New words:
- **her** — here
- **å finne / finner** — to find (finner – fant – funnet)
- **nyttig** — useful (*nytte* = benefit, use)
- **informasjon** — information
- **isolering** — insulating, insulation (the activity; *isolasjon* = the material)
- **å tette / tetting** — to seal, make airtight / sealing (*tett* = tight, dense)
- **et tak** — roof; also ceiling
- **et bygg / bygg** — building (zero plural; more technical than *bygning*)

Grammar: V2 — fronted *her* pushes the subject behind the verb (*her **finner**
du*). Verbal nouns in **-ing** are built off verbs: *isolere → isolering*,
*tette → tetting*. *til* here means "for": *tak til bygg* = roofs for buildings.

## 15

**Vi har samlet anbefalte produkter, konstruksjoner og råd til jobben.**
We have gathered recommended products, constructions and advice for the job.

New words:
- **å samle / har samlet** — to gather, collect / have gathered
- **å anbefale / anbefalte** — to recommend / recommended
- **et produkt / produkter** — product
- **en konstruksjon / konstruksjoner** — construction, structural assembly
- **en jobb / jobben** — job

Grammar: *anbefalte* is the past participle used attributively in the plural —
*et anbefalt produkt* → *anbefalte produkter*. Attributive adjectives take **-e**
in the plural and after a definite article, so it only looks like a past tense.
*Råd* is a neuter zero-plural, like *tiltak* in sentence 5.

## 16

**Skrå tretak kan deles i to hovedgrupper: tak med isolasjon plassert mellom taksperrene eller -åsene i skråtaket, hvor taket ligger over et oppvarmet loftsrom, og tak uten isolasjon over et ventilert, uoppvarmet, uisolert loftsrom.**
Pitched timber roofs can be divided into two main groups: roofs with insulation placed between the rafters or purlins of the pitched roof, where the roof sits above a heated attic space, and roofs without insulation above a ventilated, unheated, uninsulated attic space.

New words:
- **skrå** — sloping, slanting, pitched (cf. *å skråne*)
- **tretak** — timber roof
- **å dele / deles** — to divide / is divided
- **en hovedgruppe / hovedgrupper** — main group
- **hoved-** — main, chief (prefix, from *hode*)
- **å plassere / plassert** — to place / placed
- **mellom** — between
- **en taksperre / taksperrene** — rafter
- **en ås / åsene** — purlin (also: ridge, hill)
- **skråtak** — pitched roof
- **å ligge** — to lie, sit, be situated (ligger – lå – ligget)
- **over** — above, over
- **oppvarmet** — heated
- **et loft / loftsrom** — attic / attic space
- **uten** — without
- **uoppvarmet** — unheated
- **uisolert** — uninsulated
- **u-** — un- (negation prefix)

Grammar: the leading hyphen in *eller **-åsene*** stands in for the first element
of the preceding compound — read it as *takåsene*. Norwegian technical prose uses
this to avoid repeating the shared part; English must spell it out.

The **u-** prefix is the productive negation, English *un-*: *uisolert*,
*uoppvarmet*, *uvanlig*, *umulig*. It attaches to past participles freely, which
is how three stack up here — *ventilert, uoppvarmet, uisolert*, all adjectives
agreeing with neuter *loftsrom*.

## 17

**Det er de isolerte, skrå tretakene som omtales i dette kapitlet.**
It is the insulated, pitched timber roofs that are dealt with in this chapter.

New words:
- **isolert / de isolerte** — insulated / the insulated (ones)
- **å omtale / omtales** — to discuss, describe, mention / is discussed
- **et kapittel / kapitlet** — chapter (vowel drop: *kapittel* → *kapitlet*)

Grammar: a **cleft sentence** — *Det er X som …*, parallel to English "It is X
that …". It puts focus on one element; here it singles out the insulated roofs,
implicitly setting aside the uninsulated group from sentence 16. The relative
*som* is obligatory in a cleft, unlike the optional *som* in sentence 3.

Double definiteness again: *de isolerte, skrå tretakene* — article *de* **and**
suffix *-ene*, with *-e* on both adjectives.

## 18

**Disse fins i ulike varianter hvor isolasjonen enten er plassert mellom sperrene i bare deler av taket (typisk for 1½-etasjes boliger med loftromstakstoler fra forskjellige tidsperioder) eller hvor hele skråtaket er isolert fra møne til raft og hvor underliggende varme rom har skrå himling.**
These exist in various forms, where the insulation is either placed between the rafters in only parts of the roof (typical of 1½-storey houses with attic-room trusses from various periods), or where the whole pitched roof is insulated from ridge to eaves and where the heated rooms below have a sloping ceiling.

New words:
- **disse** — these
- **å finnes / fins** — to exist, be found (*fins* = short form of *finnes*)
- **ulik / ulike** — different, various, unlike
- **en variant / varianter** — variant, version
- **enten … eller** — either … or
- **en sperre / sperrene** — rafter (short for *taksperre*)
- **bare** — only (everyday counterpart of *kun*, sentence 14)
- **en del / deler** — part
- **typisk** — typical; **typisk for** — typical of
- **en etasje** — storey, floor; **1½-etasjes** — one-and-a-half-storey
- **en takstol** — roof truss
- **loftromstakstol** — attic-room truss (shaped to leave usable attic space)
- **forskjellig / forskjellige** — different, various
- **en tidsperiode / tidsperioder** — time period, era
- **hel / hele** — whole, entire
- **et møne** — ridge (of a roof)
- **et raft** — eaves
- **underliggende** — underlying, situated below
- **en himling** — ceiling (technical term; everyday speech says *tak*)

Grammar: *enten … eller* works like "either … or"; learn it beside *både … og*
(both … and) and *verken … eller* (neither … nor). The writer then chains three
relative clauses off *varianter*: *hvor … eller hvor … og hvor*.

*varme* in *underliggende varme rom* is the plural adjective "heated", not the
noun *varme* (warmth) — indefinite plural, so no article.

*Tak* covers both roof and ceiling, which is why technical writing needs
**himling** for the ceiling surface specifically. *Skrå himling* is what you get
when the pitched roof is insulated all the way from *møne* to *raft* with no
attic in between.

## 19

**Bygninger med isolasjon i skråtaket har etter hvert blitt vanlig, blant annet fordi denne byggemåten gir mulighet for full romutnyttelse av den øverste etasjen.**
Buildings with insulation in the pitched roof have gradually become common, partly because this construction method allows full use of the space in the top storey.

New words:
- **etter hvert** — gradually, over time, eventually
- **vanlig** — common, usual, ordinary
- **blant annet** (bl.a.) — among other things; here: partly, not least
- **fordi** — because
- **en byggemåte** — construction method (*bygge* + *måte*)
- **en måte** — way, manner
- **å gi / gir** — to give (gir – ga – gitt)
- **gi mulighet for** — to allow for, make possible
- **full** — full
- **romutnyttelse** — use of space, space utilisation
- **å utnytte / utnyttelse** — to utilise, exploit / utilisation
- **øverst / den øverste** — topmost / the top (one)

Grammar: *blant annet* literally "among other [things]" — in a *fordi*-clause it
signals one reason among several, hence English "partly because".

Adjective agreement: subject *bygninger* is plural, so strict agreement gives
*har blitt vanlige*. The uninflected *vanlig* reads as "has become common
practice", describing the phenomenon rather than counting buildings. Both occur
in real technical prose; *vanlige* is safer when writing.

*den øverste etasjen* — double definiteness with a superlative: *-ste* takes the
definite *-e* and needs the article *den*, like *den beste*.

(The source has a duplicated *for*: *gir mulighet for for full* — a typo.)

## 20

**Fra ca. 1980 ble det mer og mer vanlig med sperretak med isolasjon i hele skråtaket, men 1½-etasjes boliger med loftsromstakstoler (A-takstoler) er også i utstrakt bruk.**
From about 1980 it became more and more common to use rafter roofs with insulation throughout the pitched roof, but 1½-storey houses with attic-room trusses (A-trusses) are also in widespread use.

New words:
- **fra** — from
- **ca.** (*cirka*) — approximately, about
- **ble** — became (past of *å bli*)
- **mer og mer** — more and more
- **et sperretak** — rafter roof (framed with *sperrer*, no truss)
- **men** — but
- **en A-takstol** — A-truss (named for its shape)
- **også** — also, too
- **utstrakt** — extensive, widespread; **i utstrakt bruk** — in widespread use
- **bruk** — use

Grammar: **det blir vanlig med X** is a useful impersonal frame — dummy subject
*det* plus *med* introducing what became common. Literally "it became common with
rafter roofs", idiomatically "rafter roofs became the norm". Same shape in *det er
noe galt med* and *det blir problemer med*.

The fronted time phrase *Fra ca. 1980* triggers V2 — *ble det*, not *det ble*.
This inversion has now appeared with a fronted subordinate clause (2), a
prepositional phrase (10), a *for å*-phrase (12), an adverb (14) and a time
expression (here).

(The source spells it *loftsromstaksoler*, missing a *t* — read
*loftsromstakstoler*, the same word as *loftromstakstoler* in sentence 18, with or
without the linking *-s-*.)
